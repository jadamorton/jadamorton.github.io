About this home

Welcome to 8221 Anglers Edge Ct!

Nestled on a cul-de-sac in the serene community of Sloop Cove Landing, this stunning stone-front North Hampton model offers nearly 3,500 square feet of finished living space above grade, PLUS an additional 1,900 square feet in the unfinished lower level, creating nearly 5,400 square feet of total potential living space!

Set on almost half an acre and backing to a beautiful wooded setting, this home perfectly blends impressive scale, timeless details, and the convenience of main-level living.

Step inside to a dramatic two-story foyer and an open, light-filled floor plan. At the heart of the home is the oversized gourmet kitchen, featuring granite countertops, cooktop, double wall ovens, stainless steel appliances, abundant cabinetry, large pantry, and plenty of room to gather.

The kitchen flows effortlessly into the breakfast area and bright morning room, where walls of windows bring in natural light and create the perfect spot for morning coffee or simply enjoying the peaceful surroundings. A large formal dining room provides additional space for entertaining, perfect for the holidays!

The grand living room makes an immediate impression with dramatic soaring ceilings, fireplace, and oversized windows that flood the space with natural light while framing beautiful views of the wooded backdrop. The main level features two bedrooms, one offering the flexibility to serve as a guest room or executive home office, and the other a true Primary Suite. The spacious Primary Suite includes two generous walk-in closets and a private bath with a soaking tub, two separate vanities, and walk-in shower.

A convenient laundry room and powder room complete the main level. Upstairs, three additional bedrooms provide plenty of space for guests, hobbies, or whatever fits your next chapter. And there is still more room to grow! The1,900-square-foot unfinished basement offers an incredible opportunity to create additional living and entertaining space tailored completely to your needs.

Outside, enjoy the privacy of a 0.46-acre home-site backing to mature trees and woods, all from a peaceful cul-de-sac setting. An attached three-car garage provides even more space and convenience. While the setting feels tucked away, the location keeps you connected, with Routes 10 and 100 just minutes away for convenient access throughout Anne Arundel County and beyond.
