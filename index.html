All About Jada

Jada Morton is a 20-year-old journalism student at the University of Maryland. Before attending UMD, Jada was enrolled in two performing arts schools: Brooklyn Park Middle School and Annapolis High School. Throughout her time at these schools she cultivated skills in both Film and Creative Writing. The skillset she acquired in these programs fueled her desire to commit as a journalism major and apply her prior knowledge.

During her time in the Film and New Media program Jada was able to participate in all aspects of the filmmaking process. She developed skills as a script writer, producer, editor, cinematographer, photographer and director. With these opportunities she was able to create work that gained her recognition in the form of awards and showcases. Alongside the hands-on work, Jada developed valuable time management and communication skills from this experience as well.

Outside of her academics, Jada has formed a genuine passion for photography. This passion consists of photographing events and collecting small digital cameras. Jada strives to expand this passion with her commitment to journalism.
